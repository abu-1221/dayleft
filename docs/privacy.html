DayLeft privacy

DayLeft stores everything on your device. It has no accounts, no servers, no analytics and no ads.

Android app

The app has no internet permission, so it cannot send data anywhere. If you allow SMS access, it reads only debit messages that arrive while the app is installed, on the phone, to add the amount and merchant to your list. Messages are not stored or copied.

Web app

Data is kept in your browser storage. Clearing site data erases it. Use Settings, Backup to keep a copy.
