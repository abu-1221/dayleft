DayLeft privacy

DayLeft stores everything on your device. It has no accounts, no servers, no analytics and no ads.

Android app

The app has no internet permission, so it cannot send data anywhere. It does not ask for SMS access. If you share or paste a bank SMS into the app, the amount and merchant are read on the phone and the message text is not stored.

Web app

Data is kept in your browser storage. Clearing site data erases it. Use Settings, Backup to keep a copy.
